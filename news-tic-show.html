Ryōshi Brings The Forth Kind To Réunion Island

Ryōshi will present its new album on stage at Cité des Arts, alongside Bad Situation from France and Réunion's own Pamplemousse and Chili Good Good.

Ryōshi is crossing the ocean.

On Saturday 14 November, the band will present its new album, The Forth Kind, to the audience of Réunion Island at TIC SHOW, held at Cité des Arts.

The night is headlined by Bad Situation, visiting from France, and brings together some of the island's own bands: Pamplemousse and Chili Good Good will share the stage with Ryōshi for an evening of heavy, energetic music.

For Ryōshi, it's a milestone: taking the new songs beyond Mauritius, meeting a new crowd and sharing the stage with bands from two different islands and one mainland.

Four bands. Three origins.
One loud night.
