— Who We Are —

Formed in 2018, Ryōshi was born from a shared ambition: to create music without compromise and leave a lasting artistic mark beyond the boundaries of Mauritius.

Rooted in progressive metalcore, the band blends crushing riffs, atmospheric textures, technical precision and raw emotion into a sound that is both aggressive and deeply expressive. For Ryōshi, music is more than performance, it is a constant search for evolution, creativity and identity.

The name Ryōshi carries a dual meaning. In Japanese, ryoshi traditionally means fisherman. Yet the band also embraces another interpretation: ryo as excel and shi as master, a philosophy of striving for excellence while mastering one's craft through dedication and creation.

From local underground venues to regional festivals, Ryōshi's guiding principle has remained unchanged: push creativity further and leave a permanent trace on the Mauritian, regional and international music scene.

— Timeline —

Through the Years

2018 — The Beginning

Birth of a Vision

The band was formed under the name King of None by a group of friends united by a clear ambition: to establish themselves as a fully fledged force within the Mauritian music scene.

2018 — The Beginning

First Steps on Stage

The group began performing across the island, determined to present a distinctive and uncompromising sound to local audiences.

2018 — The Beginning

First Release

King of None unveiled its debut single, "Ascendence", accompanied by an official music video, the first concrete statement of the band's artistic identity.

2019 — Breakthrough

Underground Rock Festival Debut

The band made its first appearance at the Underground Rock Festival, stepping onto one of the country's major stages. It was a full-scale test and a resounding success.

2019 — Breakthrough

First International Performance

Ryōshi left Mauritian shores for the first time to perform at Nuit de Kal in Réunion Island, sharing the stage with renowned French metal act Black Bomb A.

2019 — Breakthrough

A New Identity

Upon returning to Mauritius, the band adopted the name Ryōshi, marking a major turning point in its artistic direction, musical vision and live performance philosophy.

2020 — Resilience

Creativity in Isolation

As the world was struck by the COVID-19 pandemic, live music came to a halt. Despite the distance, the members remained connected and continued composing, using the period to push their creativity even further.

2022 — Return

Back on Stage

After a long silence, Ryōshi made its long-awaited return to live performance with new material, renewed energy and a stronger sonic identity.

2023 — Expansion

"Altered Evolution"

Ryōshi released the single "Altered Evolution" alongside an official music video, further refining the band's progressive metalcore signature.

2023 — Expansion

Return to Réunion

The band travelled once again to Réunion to perform at Maytal: La Revanche, strengthening its presence on the regional scene.

2024 — New Audiences

From Underground to the Public Square

Ryōshi performed at the major Pepcity event at Tribeca Mall, bringing its music to a broader mainstream audience while staying true to its roots.

2025 — First Tour

Madagascar Tour

For the first time, Ryōshi travelled to Madagascar for a memorable three-date tour across Antananarivo, opening a new chapter in the band's international journey.

2025 — First Tour

First EP

Ryōshi released its debut five-track EP, The Hunt, a milestone that captured years of growth, experimentation and artistic evolution.

2026 — Recognition

National Recognition

Ryōshi received one of the strongest acknowledgements of its career by earning a nomination for "Live Band of the Year" at the Mauritius Music Awards.

2026 — Recognition

The Portal

A mysterious campaign began as "The Portal" was revealed. The message was clear: a new era was approaching, but the full story remained hidden.

— The Journey Continues —

From a group of friends in 2018 to a progressive metalcore act carrying Mauritian metal beyond its shores, Ryōshi continues to evolve with the same objective that ignited the project from day one: create without limits, excel through mastery, and leave a lasting mark on the musical landscape.
