The Riffs Are Back

Ryōshi joins Divoltère and Kreolyte for another night of heavy sounds and high energy.

The amps are calling again.

Not long after bringing The Forth Kind to the stage, Ryōshi is heading back to Café du Vieux Conseil for another night of heavy sounds, huge riffs and high energy.

This time, the occasion is Kas Kas Ki Lame, an event organised by Divoltère, bringing together three bands for one loud night.

Joining Divoltère on stage will be Kreolyte, alongside Ryōshi, for a lineup that promises plenty of intensity from the first note to the last.

For Ryōshi, it's another opportunity to get back in front of the crowd, turn the volume up and share the energy that only a live show can create.

Three bands. One stage.
A whole lot of riffs.

Kas Kas Ki Lame

LocationCafé du Vieux Conseil, Port-Louis

PriceRs 750

We'll see you there.

And yes, your ears have been warned.
