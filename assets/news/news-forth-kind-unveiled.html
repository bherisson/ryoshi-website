Ryōshi Unveils The Forth Kind

A new album that pushes beyond the boundaries of Ryōshi's established sound.

The wait is over.

Ryōshi officially releases The Forth Kind, marking the beginning of a new chapter in the band's musical journey.

Following the release of their first single, No Virtue, Ryōshi now unveils the complete album, featuring eight tracks that explore new territories while staying rooted in the band's identity.

The Forth Kind represents a willingness to experiment, bringing together different styles, influences and atmospheres across the album. Rather than following a single formula, Ryōshi uses these eight tracks to explore what their sound can become.

From the intensity that listeners have come to expect to unexpected shifts in style and direction, the album is an invitation to discover another side of Ryōshi.

This is not simply a continuation.
It is an evolution.

The Forth Kind is now available on Spotify and all major streaming platforms.

The transmission is complete.
Now it's time to listen.
