Ryōshi Announces The Forth Kind Album Launch

Ryōshi announces the official release of their new album and a special live launch.

After weeks of mysterious signals, unexplained artworks, and glimpses into unknown dimensions, the message has finally been decoded.

Ryōshi officially announces the release of their new album, The Forth Kind, arriving on 28 August 2026.

This new chapter will be revealed through a special album launch event at Café du Vieux Conseil, Port Louis.

A night dedicated to the arrival of a new era, featuring special performances from two powerful acts joining the journey: Amakartus and From The Storm.

More than an album release, The Forth Kind represents a new transmission from Ryōshi. A continuation of the band's exploration of sound, identity, and artistic expression.

The signals have led here.
The portal is open.

The next chapter begins on 28 August 2026.

Café du Vieux Conseil, Port Louis
Rs 700 at the door
